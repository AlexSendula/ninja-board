Planning

  • Right-click a task

    Its card opens, and stays open while you pick

  • This week, any day

    For the whole week, no particular day

  • Pick a day

    The day you'll do it

  • Pick it again

    Due that day instead, in red; once more makes it the do day again

  • Right-click a day

    Due that day, keeping a do day on another day; on the red day, the deadline goes

  • Next week, Later

    Another week, or No date yet: back to the backlog; a deadline in a week it leaves goes too

  • Plan this week

    On an upcoming week: drag from To plan onto a day, or onto Planned or due for any day

The views

  • Today

    What's on now, what's due, and the day's schedule

  • Week

    The days, the board and Coming up; ‹ › for past weeks and weeks to plan

  • Projects

    Every project by status, with its next step

  • Backlog & Waiting

    The inbox, undated tasks, someday, who you wait on
